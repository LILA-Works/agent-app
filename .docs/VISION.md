# Vision Document

This is a long-term vision document for LILA. Use this document to evaluate whether technical and product choices made fit well into this vision.

## Tenets

**Predictability over autonomy** - graph as plans, validated and dry-run-able before execution.

**Privacy first** you own your data, conversation histories, preferences.

**One agent instance per user** the agent may talk to others, but it answers you.

**Consent before consequence** tools declare whether actions are reversible, policies influence whether irreversible actions require your approval.

## What is LILA?

LILA's goal is to be a personal agent you can trust: private, local, and predictable before it acts.

## What are some use cases for LILA?

**Coding Agent** - LILA is not competing against the likes of Claude Code or Codex, but rather having enough capabilities to evolve its own ecosystem of workflows and toolkits.

**Home Assistant** - LILA would like to replace your Alexa or Google Home and operate all your smart devices for you.

**Personal Assistant** - Browse the news, manage your calendar and inbox, book a flight, LILA will do the research, present you the options, and await your decisions.

**Workflow Assistant** - Home from a photoshoot and need to postprocess your pictures (again)? LILA will learn your preferences and habits and handle that for you.

**Research Assistant** - Have a question? LILA will dig through the internet to find you the best answer.
